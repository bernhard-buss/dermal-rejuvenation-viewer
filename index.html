Read me first

With every value taken from a published source, the dose does not add up: 40,000 implanted cells secreting the measured amount of active TGF-β peak at about ng/mL, some × below the concentration that activates a fibroblast, and × below it at the least-covered point of the default lattice. That is this model's first real prediction, and the reason to measure rather than assume. What would close the gap.

Each implant site is a sphere of cells secreting one signal into dermis, at steady state; the signal decays over the length λ. The goal is as much of the dermis as possible inside the window, between the activation threshold of the aged cells and the fibrotic ceiling. Use several injection depths to fill the volume, or let Optimise search for you. Every value has a badge saying where it comes from; click it, or any ⓘ, for details and references. Quick start.
