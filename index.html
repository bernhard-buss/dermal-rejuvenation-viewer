Read me first

Each implant site is a sphere of cells secreting one signal into homogeneous dermis, at steady state; the signal decays over the length λ. The goal is as much of the dermis as possible inside the window, between the activation threshold of the aged cells and the fibrotic ceiling. Every value has a badge saying where it comes from; click it, or any ⓘ, for the details and the references. Quick start.
Bernhard Buss (master thesis), supervised by Nicholas Lawler; group of Prof. G.V. Shivashankar (ETH Zurich / PSI). Builds on Roy et al., Aging Cell 2024 (doi:10.1111/acel.14032). Prototype, not peer-reviewed. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded. References · About
